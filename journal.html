May 20, 2026

Tomato Week — Into the Soil

English

Weekly Summary

After two weeks of hardening off, the first specialty tomato starts went into the ground this week. The volcanic-rich Puyallup soil — worked through over the prior weekend — accepted the seedlings under a forgiving sky. Periodic showers kept watering needs minimal. Chokeberry plants continue to establish well. The driveway, recently full of trays, now stands empty as the seedlings find their permanent home.

Category Patterns

Transplanting dominates this week's activity, with the long arc from seed → tray → driveway → soil now complete for the earliest starts. Soil preparation, watering, and bed layout combined into a coherent move from indoor to outdoor cultivation. Salmon-fed valley soil meets greenhouse-raised seedlings.

Next Tasks

Monitor newly-planted tomatoes for transplant shock and adjust watering as the roots find the new soil. Begin staking and cage placement for vining varieties. Plant remaining starts as conditions allow. Watch the late-spring forecast — the valley still surprises in May.

Español

Resumen Semanal

Después de dos semanas de endurecimiento, los primeros inicios de tomate especiales se plantaron en la tierra esta semana. El suelo rico del Puyallup — trabajado el fin de semana anterior — recibió las plántulas bajo un cielo amable. Las lluvias intermitentes mantuvieron las necesidades de riego mínimas. Las plantas de arándano negro continúan estableciéndose bien. La entrada, recientemente llena de bandejas, ahora está vacía mientras las plántulas encuentran su hogar permanente.

Patrones por Categoría

El trasplante domina la actividad de esta semana, con el largo arco de semilla → bandeja → entrada → suelo ahora completo para los primeros inicios. La preparación del suelo, el riego y la disposición del lecho se combinaron en un movimiento coherente del cultivo interior al exterior. El suelo del valle alimentado por salmones se encuentra con plántulas criadas en invernadero.

Próximas Tareas

Monitorear los tomates recién plantados para el choque del trasplante y ajustar el riego mientras las raíces encuentran el nuevo suelo. Comenzar el estacado y la colocación de jaulas para las variedades trepadoras. Plantar los inicios restantes según las condiciones lo permitan. Seguir el pronóstico tardío de primavera — el valle aún sorprende en mayo.

May 18, 2026

Tomato Week — Replanting & Rain

English

Weekly Summary

This week focused primarily on tomato seedling management, with most starts planted and positioned for growing. A chokeberry watering and soil preparation work rounded out the week. Heavy rain on May 15th reduced irrigation needs, but later in the week potting soil was acquired and tomato replanting was undertaken to optimize growing conditions for the starts.

Category Patterns

Tomatoes dominate the activity (3 counts), reflecting the main focus of the week. Planting and seedlings each appear twice, showing the dual emphasis on establishment and propagation. Watering-related tasks total 3 counts combined, indicating consistent attention to moisture management despite the rain event.

Next Tasks

Monitor tomato plants for growth and hardening off before final transplant. Check soil moisture and establish regular watering schedule. Continue monitoring chokeberry plants and other established plantings.

May 13, 2026

Tomato Week — The Hardening Begins

English

Weekly Summary

This week focused heavily on tomato planting and establishment, with 4 tomato-related entries logged. Seedlings planted on two separate occasions and watered once early in the week. By mid-week, specialty tomato plants were positioned in the driveway — hardening off under the Puyallup sky before their final home in the ground.

Category Patterns

Tomatoes dominated all activity (4 entries). Planting appeared twice, seedlings twice. Minimal watering — either the valley rain did its work, or focus was elsewhere. A concentrated, intentional week.

Next Tasks

Continue watering as plants establish. Monitor hardening progress. Prepare final bed locations. Check remaining seedlings not yet planted.

WSU Master Gardener Sale · May 2026

The Tomato Starts

These specialty tomato starts came from the Pierce County Master Gardener Annual Plant Sale — one of the largest gardening events in the Pacific Northwest.

The Event

Held every spring at the Puyallup Fairgrounds Agriplex Building, 110 9th Ave SW, Puyallup. This year ran May 2–3, 2026. An annual event worth putting on your calendar — bring a wagon.

What's There
  • 50+ varieties of tomatoes grown by Master Gardeners
  • Vegetable and herb starts, native plants, perennials, and annuals
  • Gardening books and supplies
  • Free expert advice from WSU Extension-educated Master Gardeners
Free Classes (2026)
  • Reimagining Your Lawn
  • Right Plant, Right Place
  • Attracting Native Bees to Your Garden Oasis
Why It Matters

Proceeds support the Master Gardeners' free community education programs. It's a one-stop shop to refresh your garden, learn from experts, and connect with fellow growers in the Puyallup Valley.
